## Introduction

While kinematics describes motion geometry, dynamics describes forces and torques required to produce motion. Essential for control and simulation.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

---

## Theory Content

### Section 1: Equations of Motion

The robot dynamics equation: τ = M(q)q̈ + C(q,q̇)q̇ + G(q)

- M: Mass matrix
- C: Coriolis/centrifugal terms
- G: Gravity terms

### Section 2: Force Control

Force control maintains desired contact forces. Impedance control regulates the relationship between force and motion.

<RAGZone id="theory-content"></RAGZone>

## Simulation Lab

```python
import numpy as np
# Simple gravity compensation
mass = 2.0  # kg
g = 9.81
link_length = 0.5
angle = np.pi/4

# Torque needed to hold against gravity
torque = mass * g * link_length * np.cos(angle)
print(f"Gravity compensation torque: {torque:.2f} Nm")
```

## Quiz

### Question 1
What does dynamics describe?
A) Position only B) Forces and torques for motion C) Colors D) Sounds
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Dynamics relates forces/torques to resulting motion.</details>

### Question 2
M(q) in dynamics equation represents?
A) Velocity B) Mass/inertia matrix C) Position D) Time
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: M(q) is the configuration-dependent mass matrix.</details>

### Question 3
Force control is used for?
A) Speed only B) Contact tasks C) Navigation D) Vision
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Force control manages contact forces during manipulation.</details>

### Question 4
Gravity compensation?
A) Ignores gravity B) Applies torque to counteract gravity C) Increases gravity D) Removes mass
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Gravity compensation applies torques to hold position against gravity.</details>

### Question 5
Impedance control regulates?
A) Temperature B) Force-motion relationship C) Color D) Sound
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Impedance control manages the dynamic relationship between force and motion.</details>
