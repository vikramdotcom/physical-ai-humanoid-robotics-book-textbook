## Introduction

Beyond walking, dynamic locomotion includes running, jumping, and other movements with aerial phases where no feet touch the ground.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

---

## Theory Content

### Section 1: Aerial Phases

During aerial phases, the robot is a projectile. Only gravity acts on the CoM. Angular momentum is conserved.

### Section 2: Impact Absorption

Landing generates high forces. Compliant legs and controlled touchdown reduce peak forces and prevent damage.

### Section 3: Energy Efficiency

Elastic energy storage (springs, tendons) improves efficiency. Running can be more efficient than walking at higher speeds.

## Simulation Lab

```python
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Projectile motion (jumping)
g = 9.81
v0 = 3.0  # Initial vertical velocity
t = np.linspace(0, 2*v0/g, 100)
y = v0*t - 0.5*g*t**2

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 5))
plt.plot(t, y)
plt.xlabel('Time (s)')
plt.ylabel('Height (m)')
plt.title('Jump Trajectory (Aerial Phase)')
plt.axhline(y=0, color='k', linestyle='--')
plt.grid(True)
plt.show()
print(f"Max height: {v0**2/(2*g):.2f} m")
```

## Quiz

### Question 1
During aerial phase?
A) Both feet on ground B) Robot is a projectile C) Only walking D) No motion
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: In aerial phase, the robot follows projectile motion under gravity.</details>

### Question 2
Impact absorption prevents?
A) Movement B) High forces and damage C) Energy storage D) Running
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Controlled landing reduces peak forces that could damage the robot.</details>

### Question 3
Elastic elements help with?
A) Color B) Energy efficiency C) Weight D) Size
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Springs store and return energy, improving locomotion efficiency.</details>

### Question 4
Running vs walking?
A) Running is always slower B) Running can be more efficient at high speed C) Same efficiency D) Running uses no energy
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Running becomes more efficient than walking above certain speeds.</details>

### Question 5
Angular momentum in aerial phase?
A) Increases B) Decreases C) Is conserved D) Becomes zero
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**C**: Angular momentum is conserved during flight (no external torques).</details>
