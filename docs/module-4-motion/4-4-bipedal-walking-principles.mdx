## Introduction

Walking on two legs requires maintaining balance while moving. Key concepts include ZMP (Zero Moment Point) and capture point dynamics.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

---

## Theory Content

### Section 1: Zero Moment Point (ZMP)

ZMP is the point where the ground reaction force produces no horizontal moment. For stable walking, ZMP must stay within the support polygon.

### Section 2: Gait Cycles

Walking consists of phases: double support (both feet), single support (one foot), and swing. Gait parameters include step length, frequency, and duration.

### Section 3: Capture Point

The capture point indicates where to step to stop falling. Modern walking controllers track capture point dynamics for robust locomotion.

## Simulation Lab

```python
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Simple walking CoM trajectory
t = np.linspace(0, 4, 200)
step_length = 0.3
step_time = 0.5

# CoM moves smoothly between foot positions
com_x = step_length * t / step_time
com_y = 0.02 * np.sin(2 * np.pi * t / step_time)  # Lateral sway

plt.figure(figsize=(12, 4))
plt.subplot(1, 2, 1)
plt.plot(t, com_x)
plt.xlabel('Time (s)')
plt.ylabel('Forward position (m)')
plt.title('CoM Forward Motion')

plt.subplot(1, 2, 2)
plt.plot(t, com_y)
plt.xlabel('Time (s)')
plt.ylabel('Lateral position (m)')
plt.title('CoM Lateral Sway')
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
```

## Quiz

### Question 1
ZMP stands for?
A) Zero Motor Power B) Zero Moment Point C) Zone Motion Planning D) Zigzag Motion Path
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Zero Moment Point is where ground reaction produces no horizontal moment.</details>

### Question 2
For stable walking, ZMP must be?
A) Outside support polygon B) Within support polygon C) At robot center D) Moving constantly
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: ZMP inside support polygon prevents tipping.</details>

### Question 3
Double support phase means?
A) Two robots B) Both feet on ground C) Moving twice as fast D) Double the force
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Double support is when both feet contact the ground simultaneously.</details>

### Question 4
Capture point indicates?
A) Camera location B) Where to step to stop falling C) Enemy position D) Battery level
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: The capture point shows where to place the foot to arrest a fall.</details>

### Question 5
Gait cycle includes?
A) Only standing B) Support and swing phases C) Only jumping D) Only running
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Walking gait alternates between support phases and swing phases.</details>
