## Theory Content

### Section 1: Safety Requirements

ISO 15066 defines force/pressure limits for safe human contact. Robots must detect and respond to collisions.

### Section 2: Compliant Control

Impedance control makes robots yield to external forces, enabling safe physical interaction.

### Section 3: Collaborative Manipulation

Humans and robots can jointly manipulate objects, combining human intelligence with robot strength.

## Quiz

### Question 1
pHRI stands for?
A) Physical Human-Robot Interface B) Physical Human-Robot Interaction C) Power Human Robot Input D) Precision Handling Robot Integration
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Physical Human-Robot Interaction covers direct physical contact between humans and robots.</details>

### Question 2
ISO 15066 defines?
A) Colors B) Safe force/pressure limits C) Network protocols D) Power requirements
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: ISO 15066 specifies limits for safe collaborative robot operation.</details>

### Question 3
Compliant control enables?
A) Rigid behavior B) Yielding to external forces C) Higher speed D) Lower cost
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Compliance allows robots to give way safely when contacted.</details>

### Question 4
Cobots are designed for?
A) Isolation B) Human collaboration C) Maximum speed D) No interaction
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Collaborative robots (cobots) work alongside humans safely.</details>

### Question 5
Force limiting prevents?
A) Motion B) Injury from excessive force C) Power use D) Communication
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Force limits prevent harmful impacts with humans.</details>
