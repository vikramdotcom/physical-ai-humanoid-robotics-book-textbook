# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to the comprehensive textbook on **Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics**. This book is designed for high school students (grades 9-11) and early college learners who want to understand the exciting field where artificial intelligence meets physical robots.

## What You'll Learn

This textbook covers the complete journey from foundational concepts to advanced applications:

- **Module 1: Foundations** - What is Physical AI and how did we get here?
- **Module 2: Robot Anatomy** - How are humanoid robots built?
- **Module 3: Perception** - How do robots see and sense their world?
- **Module 4: Motion** - How do robots move and maintain balance?
- **Module 5: Manipulation** - How do robots grasp and interact with objects?
- **Module 6: Learning** - How do robots learn from experience?
- **Module 7: Integration** - How do we build complete robotic systems?
- **Module 8: Applications** - Where is humanoid robotics going?

## How to Use This Book

Each chapter follows a structured format designed for effective learning:

1. **Learning Objectives** - What you'll be able to do after completing the chapter
2. **Theory Content** - Core concepts with clear explanations and examples
3. **Simulation Lab** - Hands-on exercises using Google Colab
4. **Hardware Lab** - Optional physical robotics exercises using ROS2 and Jetson
5. **Case Study** - Real-world applications of the concepts
6. **Quiz** - Test your understanding with multiple-choice questions

## Prerequisites

- Basic mathematics (algebra and geometry)
- Familiarity with computers and basic programming concepts
- Curiosity about robotics and AI!

## Getting Started

Begin with [Module 1: Foundations of Physical AI](./module-1-foundations/1-1-what-is-physical-ai) to understand what physical AI is and why it matters. Each module builds on previous ones, so we recommend following them in order.
