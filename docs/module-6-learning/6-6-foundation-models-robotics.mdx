## Theory Content

### Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
Models like CLIP understand both images and text, enabling language-conditioned robot control.

### RT-X and Generalist Policies
Google's RT-X trains on diverse robot data for policies that generalize across robots and tasks.

### Language as Interface
Natural language instructions enable flexible, general-purpose robot control.
