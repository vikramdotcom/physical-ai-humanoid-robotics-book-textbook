## Introduction

The goal of robot learning is generalization—performing well on new tasks, objects, and environments not seen during training.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

## Theory Content

### Transfer Learning
Leverage knowledge from one task/domain to improve learning in another.

### Few-Shot Adaptation
Quickly adapt to new situations with minimal additional data.

### Emergent Behaviors
Complex behaviors that arise from simple learning rules at scale.

## Quiz

### Question 1
Generalization means?
A) Training more B) Performing well on new situations C) Memorizing data D) No learning
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Generalization is success on unseen scenarios.</details>

### Question 2
Transfer learning uses?
A) No prior knowledge B) Knowledge from related tasks C) Random initialization D) Hardware transfer
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Transfer learning leverages prior learned knowledge.</details>

### Question 3
Few-shot adaptation requires?
A) Massive data B) Minimal new data C) No data D) Only simulation
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Few-shot methods adapt with very little new data.</details>

### Question 4
Emergent behaviors arise from?
A) Manual programming B) Scale and simple rules C) Hardware design D) No learning
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Complex behaviors emerge from scaled simple learning.</details>

### Question 5
Open-world robotics faces?
A) Closed environments B) Unknown, changing situations C) Only known objects D) No challenges
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Open-world means handling novel, unpredictable situations.</details>
