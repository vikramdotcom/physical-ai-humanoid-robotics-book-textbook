## Introduction

Humanoid robots are entering manufacturing and logistics, performing tasks in environments designed for humans.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

## Theory Content

### Factory Automation
Humanoids can work alongside humans on assembly lines, handling varied tasks without retooling.

### Warehouse Logistics
Picking, packing, and transporting goods in distribution centers.

### Assembly Applications
Complex assembly tasks requiring dexterity and flexibility.

## Quiz

### Question 1
Why humanoids in factories?
A) Cheaper than arms B) Work in human-designed spaces C) Faster always D) No reason
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Humanoids can use existing human workspaces and tools.</details>

### Question 2
Warehouse robots handle?
A) Only storage B) Picking and transport C) Only cleaning D) No tasks
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Warehouse robots pick, pack, and transport goods.</details>

### Question 3
Assembly requires?
A) No dexterity B) Dexterity and precision C) Only strength D) Only speed
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Assembly needs both dexterity and precision.</details>

### Question 4
Amazon uses robots for?
A) Nothing B) Warehouse automation C) Only office work D) Only delivery
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Amazon extensively automates warehouses with robots.</details>

### Question 5
Humanoid advantage over fixed robots?
A) Always faster B) Flexibility in varied tasks C) Always cheaper D) No advantage
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Humanoids can adapt to varied tasks without retooling.</details>
