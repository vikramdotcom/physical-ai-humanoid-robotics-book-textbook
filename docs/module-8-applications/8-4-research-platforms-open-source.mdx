Platforms like NASA Valkyrie, DLR David, and university robots advance the field.

### Open-Source Hardware

### Question 1
Open-source benefits include?
A) Nothing B) Collaboration and accessibility C) Higher cost D) Closed development
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Open source enables collaboration and reduces barriers.</details>

### Question 2
ROS2 is?
A) Proprietary B) Open-source middleware C) A robot D) Hardware only
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: ROS2 is open-source robot middleware.</details>

### Question 3
Research platforms enable?
A) Only commercial use B) Scientific advancement C) No research D) Only sales
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Research platforms advance scientific understanding.</details>

### Question 4
Community contribution?
A) Not allowed B) Accelerates development C) Slows progress D) Only bugs
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Community contributions accelerate open-source development.</details>

### Question 5
Why share robot designs?
A) Required by law B) Enables replication and improvement C) No benefit D) Only cost
