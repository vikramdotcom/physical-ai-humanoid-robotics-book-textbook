## Introduction

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

## Theory Content

### Assistive Robots
Help people with disabilities perform daily tasks independently.

### Elderly Care
Support for aging populations with mobility, monitoring, and companionship.

### Rehabilitation
Physical therapy robots help patients recover from injury or surgery.

Assistive robots help?

<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Assistive robots help people with disabilities live independently.</details>

### Question 2
Elderly care robots provide?
A) Nothing B) Support and monitoring C) Only entertainment D) Only cleaning
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Elderly care robots assist with various daily needs.</details>

### Question 3
Rehabilitation robots help with?
A) Diagnosis only B) Physical therapy C) Only surgery D) No medical purpose
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Rehab robots assist in physical therapy and recovery.</details>

### Question 4
Why robots for aging populations?
A) Not needed B) Growing elderly population, limited caregivers C) Only cost D) Fashion
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Demographics create demand for care assistance.</details>
