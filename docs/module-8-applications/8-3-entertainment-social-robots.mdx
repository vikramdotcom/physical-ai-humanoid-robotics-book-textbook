## Quiz

### Question 1
Companion robots provide?
A) Only work B) Emotional support C) Only cleaning D) Only security
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Companion robots offer emotional support and companionship.</details>

### Question 2
Telepresence robots enable?
A) Teleportation B) Remote presence C) Time travel D) Only video
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Telepresence allows being present remotely through a robot.</details>

### Question 3
The uncanny valley affects?
A) Hardware B) Human comfort with near-human robots C) Power systems D) Network
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Almost-but-not-quite human robots can be unsettling.</details>

### Question 4
AIBO is a?
A) Car B) Robot dog C) Phone D) Camera
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Sony AIBO is a robotic companion pet.</details>

### Question 5
Social robots need?
A) Only strength B) Emotional expression capabilities C) Only speed D) No interaction
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Social robots must express and perceive emotions.</details>
