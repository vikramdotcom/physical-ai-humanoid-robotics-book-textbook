## Introduction

Proprioception—knowing the state of one's own body—is essential for robot control. Internal sensors measure joint positions, velocities, forces, and body orientation.

<RAGZone id="introduction"></RAGZone>

---

## Theory Content

### Section 1: Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs)

IMUs combine accelerometers and gyroscopes to measure linear acceleration and angular velocity. They enable balance control and orientation estimation.

### Section 2: Encoders

Encoders measure joint angles and velocities. Types include optical (high precision) and magnetic (robust). Essential for position control.

### Section 3: Force/Torque Sensors

Force sensors enable contact detection, force control, and safe human-robot interaction. Six-axis sensors measure full force/torque vectors.

## Simulation Lab

```python
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Simulate IMU gyroscope integration for orientation
dt = 0.01
time = np.arange(0, 10, dt)
true_angle = np.sin(time)  # True orientation
gyro_rate = np.cos(time) + np.random.normal(0, 0.1, len(time))  # Noisy rate

# Integrate gyroscope
estimated_angle = np.cumsum(gyro_rate) * dt

plt.figure(figsize=(12, 4))
plt.plot(time, true_angle, 'b-', label='True angle')
plt.plot(time, estimated_angle, 'r--', label='Integrated gyro (drifts)')
plt.xlabel('Time (s)')
plt.ylabel('Angle (rad)')
plt.legend()
plt.title('IMU Gyroscope Drift')
plt.show()
```

## Quiz

### Question 1
What does an IMU measure?
A) Distance B) Acceleration and rotation rate C) Temperature D) Color
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: IMUs measure linear acceleration and angular velocity.</details>

### Question 2
Why do gyroscopes drift?
A) Battery depletion B) Integration of noise over time C) Temperature D) Magnetic interference
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Small noise errors accumulate when integrating angular rate to get angle.</details>

### Question 3
What do encoders measure?
A) Force B) Joint angle/position C) Temperature D) Current
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Encoders measure rotational position of joints.</details>

### Question 4
Force/torque sensors enable?
A) Vision B) Safe contact and force control C) Navigation D) Communication
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Force sensors enable robots to safely interact with objects and people.</details>

### Question 5
Why is proprioception important for balance?
A) Seeing obstacles B) Knowing body orientation and motion C) Communication D) Power management
<details><summary>Answer</summary>**B**: Balance requires knowing current body state to make corrections.</details>
