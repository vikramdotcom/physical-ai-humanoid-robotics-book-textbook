## Theory Content

### Gazebo
Open-source simulator with ROS2 integration. Good for navigation and manipulation.

### NVIDIA Isaac Sim
High-fidelity simulation with GPU acceleration. Excellent for AI training.

### Digital Twins
Virtual replicas synchronized with physical robots for monitoring and prediction.
